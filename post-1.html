The Cat Was Never in the Box

An opening dispatch from the lab, and a promise to show my working.

News23 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

Every journal needs a first entry, and this is mine. I have spent a long time staring at one equation, and I have come to suspect it is not telling the whole story. This space is where I will lay out that suspicion, piece by piece, so anyone can follow the reasoning and tell me where I have gone wrong.

iℏ ∂ψ/∂t = Ĥψ The equation in question

The plan is simple. I will write up each stage of the work as it happens: the ideas that survive, the ones that do not, and the late-night detours in between. Some entries will be news from the project. Others will be personal, because research is done by a person, and that person has opinions about coffee.

Why a journal?

Because arguments deserve a paper trail. If I am right, the record will show how I got there. If I am wrong, it will show exactly where, which is nearly as valuable.

The point is not to be certain. The point is to be checkable.

What comes next

Expect regular updates, a reading list for the sceptical, and a few essays that have nothing to do with physics at all. Thank you for reading, and please do keep me honest.
