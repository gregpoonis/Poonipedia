Fish to Human: A Unified Ichthyo-Anthropic Theory

On the vertebrate water-to-land transition and its downstream consequences for Sumatra, Antarctica, the artichoke, lunch and the meaning of life.

News26 Nov 2026 · 14 min read

Abstract

We present an integrative, phylogenetically unconstrained and wholly peer-unreviewed account of the ichthyo-anthropic transition. A piecewise demographic integration yields approximately 1.14 × 108 generations since Tiktaalik-grade ancestry. We couple this to a monoexponential desiccation model, the thermodynamics of an equatorial ice dwelling, a logistic model of Antarctic settlement, a density-dependent ape–artichoke interaction and a first-order grief kinetic for the McSmart Meal. Their synthesis gives the first numerical estimate of the meaning of life: ℳ = 300, in units which we confirm. The models and the Berginese are all real; Section 10 separates fact from invention.

1. Introduction

Every human is, in the cladistic sense, an extraordinarily modified lobe-finned vertebrate. Orthodox palaeobiology treats this as sufficient. We do not, because it fails to explain artichokes. The present monograph therefore proposes the Unified Ichthyo-Anthropic Theory (UIAT), under which morphological innovation, polar migration, horticultural yield and fast-food bereavement are coupled manifestations of one underlying process: the organism’s progressive, and frequently ill-advised, departure from water.

2. From fin to forearm

Figure 1 gives the chronostratigraphic scaffold. Tiktaalik, dated to roughly 375 million years ago, combines scales, gills and fin rays with a mobile neck, robust ribs and a weight-bearing fin skeleton. It is best regarded as a close relative of the lineage that led to four-limbed animals, not as a confirmed direct ancestor.

The appendicular homology in Figure 2 is the most elegant datum in this paper, chiefly because we did not invent it. Lobe-finned fish and humans share a proximal-to-distal skeletal logic of one bone, then two, then a cluster of smaller elements. Fin rays and human fingers, however, are not simple one-for-one equivalents.

3. Generational mechanics

To estimate the number of reproductive iterations separating a 375-million-year-old fish-grade ancestor from the present reader, we integrate over six intervals, each with an assumed mean generation time τi:

Ngen = Σi Δti / τi ≈ 1.14 × 108 Eq. 1 · Piecewise generational integral

Concurrently, the organism undergoes phylogenetic desiccation. Taking whole-body water fractions of 75% for the ancestral fish and 60% for the modern human (illustrative values) and assuming exponential decay:

λ = ln(75/60) / 375 Ma ≈ 5.95 × 10−4 Ma−1,  t½ = ln 2 / λ ≈ 1,165 Ma Eq. 2 · The Poonis Dampness Constant

The half-life of vertebrate moistness is thus about 1.17 billion years. Extrapolation predicts that our descendants will reach 1% water in about 7.3 billion years, at which point they will be, in technical terms, a cracker.

4. The Berginese Inuit of Sumatra

The empirical foundation of the UIAT comes from the Berginese, an Inuit tribe resident in the equatorial highlands of Sumatra. By their own account they arrived by following a very confused fish. They maintain a continuous oral record of the “Fish-Uncle” (Avunculus piscinus), a transitional relative who attended family gatherings but would not come fully indoors. They also maintain an igloo, at an ambient 27 °C, as a reference standard.

The igloo is a hemispherical shell of inner radius 1.5 m and wall thickness 0.3 m. Approximating conduction across its inner area A = 2πr² ≈ 14.1 m² with ice conductivity k = 2.2 W m−1 K−1, and generously assuming a 27 K difference across the wall:

Q̇ = kAΔT / d = (2.2 × 14.14 × 27) / 0.3 ≈ 2,799 W Eq. 3 · Equatorial igloo heat ingress (planar approximation)

The shell holds about 5.15 m³ of ice, or 4,719 kg at 917 kg m−3. With a latent heat of fusion of 334 kJ kg−1:

tmelt = mLf / Q̇ = (4,719 × 334,000) / 2,799 ≈ 5.6 × 105 s ≈ 6.5 days Eq. 4 · The Weekly Igloo (wall thinning, sun and rain neglected)

The Berginese consequently rebuild every six days and rest on the seventh, which is spent standing in a puddle. The Council of Elders has reviewed Equation 4 and described it as “optimistic”.

5. The colonisation of Antarctica

Faced with a chronic ice deficit, the Berginese proposed the obvious remedy: colonise Antarctica. The minimum meridional distance from the equator to a coast near 66° S, on a sphere of radius 6,371 km, is:

d = R⊕ Δφ = 6,371 × (66π/180) ≈ 7,339 km Eq. 5 · Lower bound on the Great Ice Pilgrimage

At a sustained raft velocity of 2 km h−1, the passage takes about 153 days, which is some 23 consecutive igloo lifetimes. The expedition therefore carried no igloo, only the memory of one.

Settlement of the colony, New Bergin, follows logistic growth from a founding party of P0 = 40 towards a carrying capacity K = 1,000, limited by the supply of dry socks, with r = 0.08 yr−1:

P(t) = K / [1 + ((K − P0) / P0) e−rt] Eq. 6 · New Bergin population; K/2 is reached at t = ln 24 / r ≈ 39.7 yr

6. Apes and artichoke cultivation

The transition from fin to grasping hand eventually produced the apes, and with them a previously uncharacterised agronomic stressor. We model yield Y of the globe artichoke (Cynara cardunculus var. scolymus) as a function of local ape density a with a Hill-type suppression term, reflecting trampling, pilfering and critical staring:

Y(a) = Y0 / [1 + (a/a50)²] Eq. 7 · Simian yield suppression (Y₀ = 12 t/ha, a₅₀ = 50 apes/km²)

7. The devastating loss of the McSmart Meal

We now turn, with appropriate solemnity, to the McSmart Meal. In Australia it sold for $6.95. In June 2026, McDonald’s announced that from 1 July the offer would become a three-tier range: a $6.95 app-only Saver with a different line-up, a $7.95 McSmart and a $9.95 McSmart Plus. The $6.95 McSmart as the species had known it was no more. The nominal price differential between the old meal and the new one bearing its name (whose contents also differ) is:

Δp / p0 = (7.95 − 6.95) / 6.95 ≈ 14.4% Eq. 9 · The Bereavement Coefficient

One dollar per week amounts to $52 per year. The psychological burden is modelled as first-order decay from an initial grief index of 100 towards an irreducible floor, with time constant τg = 60 days:

G(t) = G∞ + (G0 − G∞) e−t/τg,  G(148) ≈ 19.5 Eq. 10 · McGrief kinetics (G₀ = 100, G∞ = 12)

Evolution spent some 114 million generations equipping this primate with hands capable of holding a small fries, a small drink and a cheeseburger, and then changed the terms. No adaptive response has yet been observed, other than downloading the app.

8. The meaning of life

Philosophy has pursued the meaning of life for millennia without once specifying its units. We rectify this. Let meaning ℳ be the maximum Ape–Artichoke Product per dollar of irrecoverable lunch:

ℳ = W(a*) / Δp = 300 / 1.00 = 300 Eq. 11 · The Meaning of Life, in ape·tonnes·ha⁻¹·km⁻²·AUD⁻¹

Dimensional analysis reveals that these units cannot be reduced to anything, which we take as strong evidence that the quantity is fundamental. Note also that ℳ diverges as Δp → 0: had the price never changed, life would have been infinitely meaningful. Figure 6 summarises the full causal architecture.
